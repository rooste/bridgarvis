Juujarvis email

Juujarvis sends email from its own address on this domain. It sends one-to-one messages only: it does not send marketing, newsletters or bulk mail, and it has no mailing lists.

What Juujarvis sends

  • Replies to household members who emailed Juujarvis.
  • Messages to a specific person that a household member asked Juujarvis to send on their behalf, for example a reply to a teacher, a coach or a service provider.

Volume is low: typically a few emails per week.

If you received an email from Juujarvis

It was sent because a member of the household asked for it, or in reply to them. To stop receiving emails from Juujarvis, reply with stop or write to __CONTACT_EMAIL__. Your address will not be emailed again.

Delivery and privacy

Email is sent through Amazon Web Services with DKIM signing. Bounced addresses and complaints are recorded and those addresses are not emailed again. See the privacy policy and terms.
