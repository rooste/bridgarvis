Juujarvis family assistant

Juujarvis is a personal assistant for one family. It helps family members with calendars, reminders, tasks and everyday questions, and it can send them messages, for example appointment reminders or answers to questions they asked.

Juujarvis is not a commercial service. It does not sell anything, does not send marketing or promotional messages, and only communicates with members of the household who have agreed to it.

Calendars and reminders
Keeps track of appointments, school events and deadlines, and reminds the right person in time.
Tasks and questions
Helps with to-do lists, family logistics and everyday questions.
Where the family already is
Reached by iMessage, email or text message. No app to install.

About the name

Juujarvis is named after Juujärvi, a lake in Finnish Lapland where the family has its roots. On clear winter nights the northern lights move over the fells and the lake, just like in the picture above.

How family members reach Juujarvis

Family members talk to Juujarvis by iMessage, email or text message (SMS). Text messaging and email are described in detail on the text messaging and email pages, including how to opt in and how to stop messages.
