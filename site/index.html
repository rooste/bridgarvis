Juujarvis assistant

Juujarvis is an invitation-only personal assistant. It helps its users with calendars, reminders, tasks and everyday questions, and it can send them messages, for example appointment reminders or answers to questions they asked.

Juujarvis does not sell anything and does not send marketing or promotional messages. It only communicates with invited users who have agreed to it.

Calendars and reminders
Keeps track of appointments, events and deadlines, and sends reminders in time.
Tasks and questions
Helps with to-do lists, planning and everyday questions.
Where you already are
Reached by iMessage, email or text message. No app to install.

About the name

Juujarvis is named after Juujärvi, a lake in Finnish Lapland. On clear winter nights the northern lights move over the fells and the lake, just like in the picture above.

How users reach Juujarvis

Users talk to Juujarvis by iMessage, email or text message (SMS). Text messaging and email are described in detail on the text messaging and email pages, including how to opt in and how to stop messages.
