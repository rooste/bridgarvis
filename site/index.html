Juujarvis family assistant

Juujarvis is a personal assistant for one family. It helps family members with calendars, reminders, tasks and everyday questions, and it can send them messages, for example appointment reminders or answers to questions they asked.

Juujarvis is not a commercial service. It does not sell anything, does not send marketing or promotional messages, and only communicates with members of the household who have agreed to it.

How family members reach Juujarvis

Family members talk to Juujarvis by iMessage, email or text message (SMS). Text messaging is described in detail on the text messaging page, including how to opt in and opt out.
