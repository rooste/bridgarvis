Juujarvis text messaging (SMS)

Family members can text Juujarvis and receive replies and reminders by text message. The Juujarvis number is not published: the operator gives it directly to each family member.

Who receives messages

Only members of the household who have opted in. Messages are never sent to anyone else, and the number is not used for marketing.

How to opt in

The operator tells each family member about Juujarvis text messaging in person and gives them the number. To opt in, the family member texts JOIN to that number and receives this confirmation:

Juujarvis: You are subscribed to Juujarvis family assistant messages (reminders and replies). Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

What kind of messages

  • Replies to questions you text to Juujarvis
  • Reminders you or your family asked for, for example: Juujarvis: Dentist tomorrow at 3:00 PM. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Family notifications, for example: Juujarvis: Soccer practice moved to 6:00 PM today.

Message frequency varies with use, typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

  • Text HELP for help, or email __CONTACT_EMAIL__.
  • Text STOP at any time to stop all messages. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages.
  • Text JOIN again to opt back in.
